Wallbreakers

Lead fire, water, earth and air across the field. Bring the wall down before your squad falls.

  • Click or drag to select units, then click the ground to move them.
  • Click an enemy to focus it with ranged units. Melee units keep hitting the wall.
  • Right-click cancels the selection. Space pauses.
New games start with 500 of each crystal.
