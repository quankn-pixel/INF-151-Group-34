## Magic Spell Mechanism

- The player can discard their entire inventory to purchase one spell.
- The nature of the spell is determined by the balancing bar.
- If the majority of the bar is element X, the player receives an X spell.

## Layer 1: One Element

- All UI elements are hidden until discovered.
- Layer number = number of elements.
- The balancing bar appears after the first reset, once the player has 2 or more elements.

**Four options to investigate** (each unlocks a single element):
- Investigate the Breeze
- Investigate the Puddle
- Investigate the Patch of Dirt
- Investigate the Embers
